## /execute
- 完整语法: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Commands/execute
- 子命令速查: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Commands/execute#Syntax
- 常用子命令:
  - `as` `at` `positioned` `rotated` `align` `anchored`
  - `if` / `unless` (block, blocks, data, entity, predicate, score, etc.)
  - `store` (result, success)
  - `run`
  - `summon` (子命令)
